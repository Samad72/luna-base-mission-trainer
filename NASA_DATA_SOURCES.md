IMPORTANT DATA INTERPRETATION
DONKI provides space-weather observations, analyses, forecasts, and notifications. The app only uses returned FLR/CME records as contextual evidence that space-weather activity is monitored. It does not calculate radiation dose or predict hazards at a Moon/Mars outpost. The in-game “preparedness drill” and resource penalties are fictional, simplified educational assumptions.
