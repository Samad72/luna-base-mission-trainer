# Seven-slide project demonstration

1. **Title & introduction** — LUNA BASE: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer; one-line mission: keep a lunar/Martian outpost alive for seven simulated days.
2. **Problem & challenge** — Young learners rarely experience the connected trade-offs among life support, power, food and radiation protection.
3. **Our solution** — A browser game with five resource meters, one daily action, consumption, events, and mission outcomes.
4. **How it works** — Choose Moon/Mars, select an action, advance a day, inspect resource changes, and complete or fail the mission.
5. **NASA data connection** — NASA DONKI FLR and CME records provide recent space-weather context; explain clearly that this is not a local Moon/Mars radiation forecast.
6. **Technology, AI use & validation** — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Fetch API, DONKI; AI-assisted code/design/documentation disclosed; team tested and reviewed output.
7. **Impact, limitations & next steps** — Systems thinking and STEM engagement; simplified gameplay values; future work: stronger science explanations, accessibility, more outpost scenarios and teacher mode.
